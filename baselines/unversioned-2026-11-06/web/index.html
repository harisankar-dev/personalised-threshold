    ABOUT THE SCORE

    A transparent screen, not a consumer verdict.

    The 0–10 number combines privacy cues and high-pressure wording, capped at 10. It is not a probability or a measured reaction. The supplied dataset has no consumer intrusiveness ratings. Image analysis reads printed words; it does not judge the visual layout or whether an ad obstructs a page. Describe visual details you want considered.
