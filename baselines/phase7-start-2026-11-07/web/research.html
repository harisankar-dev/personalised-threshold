04 · BROADER RESEARCH OBJECTIVE

Five-level personalization threshold

Insufficient evidence

The levels are a conceptual decision framework. The available secondary data do not measure valid engagement and direct intrusion responses at every level on the same exposure.

    Current decision: ——Needed for validation: direct intrusion labels, engagement outcomes, per-level exposure variation and held-out validation on compatible records. No level is recommended.
