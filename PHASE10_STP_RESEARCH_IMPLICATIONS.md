# Phase 10 — bounded STP research implications

**No new Phase 10 consumer-effect or prediction result exists.** The proposed OSF individual-level analysis stopped at the rights and privacy gate. The [published location-targeting study](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/00218499.2026.2709216) and the earlier [Phase 8 descriptive inspection](PHASE8_SECONDARY_EVIDENCE_SEARCH.md) motivate questions; they do not supply a validated campaign policy for 49-C.

| STP decision | Supported now | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Segmentation | Separate existing survey profiles describe privacy/relevance or benefit responses **within their own samples**. | No same-person linkage to a marketer audience, no customer comfort prediction and no consent inference. |
| Targeting | The cited experiments vary **location specificity and disclosure** in 2×2 designs; the earlier cell means suggest responses may depend on context. | Study 1's no/city contrast differs from Study 2's country/city contrast. Neither measures all five 49-C levels or every targeting data source. |
| Positioning | A clearly worded disclosure or less revealing creative is a testable hypothesis. The current app can flag pressure and privacy cues for review. | No new experiment establishes that editing an arbitrary uploaded image reduces consumer-rated intrusion or raises conversions. The 0–10 score is an editorial rubric. |
| Budget | Preserve **A** keep current campaign; **B** revise one creative; **C** adjust eligible targeting; **D** test one extra creative; **E** consider multiple variants if justified. Compare marketer-entered costs, headroom and hypothetical break-even conversions. | No measured incremental conversion or profit uplift from the OSF lab ratings. Extra variants and segmentation are not assumed profitable. |

Personal relevance and privacy intrusion may coexist; whether they rise together for the **same respondent under the same assigned exposure** is a question for an authorized individual-level analysis. Phase 8's cell means cannot establish individual covariance or a causal mediation pathway. Lab click intention is not actual clicking, and Study 3's aggregate clicks cannot be joined to lab intrusion ratings. A permission flag, a survey concern score and a specific ad's perceived intrusion are different constructs.

The current Mode A marketer planner remains a research-informed, fixed-budget, minimum-change **advisory prototype**. Mode B remains an offline schema validator, with no operational customer upload. Any eventual narrow location-response model would remain research-only until independent creative-family, channel, audience, consent and decision-value validation. The five-level threshold is unsupported and must continue abstaining with `insufficient_evidence`.
