**Audit completed 10 November 2026.** No primary participants or respondent files were used. The source-ad images and CSV remain in the local Downloads folder and are excluded from Git; only source hashes, selected creative IDs, project-authored labels, code and derived results are committed. A dataset-page Creative Commons listing is recorded without asserting ownership of every third-party brand creative.

## Actual selected-source inspection

Downloaded publisher files: `Advertising Image Dataset for Visual Analysis.csv` (194,499 bytes, SHA-256 `cd5ad685312e6e365557166ad4b6d8e7a64825519c6edcb0044dfa0de7a8d0ca`) and `ad_images.zip` (36,721,910 bytes, SHA-256 `b397f56216094a616a8e2b7a4b1af84afaf4155447d6f51801a9304406f8337c`). ZIP members have no absolute or `..` traversal paths. All 605 JPGs decode. The CSV's `ad1.jpg`–`ad605.jpg` sequence corresponds by numeric ID to archive `Datasets/ad (1).jpg`–`ad (605).jpg`; selected screenshots were spot-checked visually. The publisher's 606 count is therefore **not** the downloaded unique-file count.

CSV columns: `ad_image`, `score`, `color`, `contrast`, `text`, `layout`, `cta`. There are no blank cells in these seven columns and no duplicate full rows; seven `text` entries are the literal placeholder `none`, not readable transcription. There are **521 unique text strings**, with **84 repeat rows beyond first** across 48 duplicate-text groups. Nine exact-image duplicate groups contain ten duplicate files beyond first. Image dimensions vary; 413/605 are 600 × 600. A simple Arabic-versus-Latin character count classifies 523 records as Arabic-dominant and 82 as Latin-dominant; this is a script audit, not verified language annotation. The selected 25 creative images and recorded text strings have no exact duplicates; their minimum sampled 64-bit difference-hash distance was 14, a limited near-duplicate screen rather than proof of no semantic duplicates.

No source field verifies product category, platform per creative, targeting method, exposure, consent, observed engagement or perceived intrusiveness. The publisher's 0–10 **visual quality** score is unrelated to 49-C's 0–10 editorial cue score. Its CTA label is not an urgency-cue ground truth. The source describes images as real social advertisements, but original placement/authenticity for each creative was not independently established. Treat generalization to other markets, English language, privacy-oriented campaigns or consumers as unsupported.

## Reuse decision

Only an internal, attributed analysis of a small non-random subset is supported. The repository stores no source image/CSV archive, no respondent data and no full-text training corpus. Source retrieval, hashes, label protocol and reproducible scripts are documented in [the evaluation](PHASE12_REAL_AD_EVALUATION.md). Any downstream publication or product redistribution of original creative images requires a separate rights review despite the dataset-page CC BY listing.
