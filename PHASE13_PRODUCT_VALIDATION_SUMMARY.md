# Phase 13 product validation summary

**Status: working local STP advisory prototype; English image screening remains a human-review aid.** Phase 13 added a conditional second local OCR pass and explicit partial/garbled warnings, stopped treating seller phone/email as viewer-specific data by default, detected common time-limited offer wording, and retained existing budget and permission decisions. The rubric score is an editorial cue count capped for display at 10, not a measured perception score.

The six reserved real ads improved exact checked-phrase recovery from 4/18 to 6/18 and removed one seller-email false high alert. However, error on two complete headline crops **worsened** (micro CER 0.2381→0.6032; WER 0.6364→1.0000), and small rotated, photographic and vertical text was often missed. There were no real urgency or viewer-identification positives in the final split, so positive cue recall and real-ad deadline recovery remain unevaluated. Synthetic deadline and identity tests and all 21 real-browser interaction checks passed; they show the software flow works, not that consumer-level accuracy is established.

The full documented R-backed regression ran **102 tests, all passing**, versus 93 pre-change. The same 604 protected files remained hash-identical. The application kept source/consent holds, budget headroom and minimum-change logic. The five-level threshold continues `insufficient_evidence`; Mode B remains offline only. Native `pyreadstat` remains Windows-blocked, with the test-only R alternative documented in Phase 12.

**Most useful Phase 14 direction:** obtain reuse-cleared, independently transcribed English real ads with enough actual deadline, seller-contact and viewer-specific privacy positives; then compare line or region-level OCR selection with the current dual-pass output on a new split. If the goal is consumer intrusiveness prediction, a separate eligible secondary dataset must link assigned personalization, exposed creative, direct perceived intrusiveness and engagement in the same respondents. No amount of OCR tuning substitutes for that measurement.
