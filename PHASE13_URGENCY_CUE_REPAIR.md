# Phase 13 time-pressure cue repair

Phase 12's reserved real-ad diagnostic contained two explicit limited-time positives; the original text regex missed both. The existing `urgent_call_to_action` cue now also recognizes limited-time offers/deals/sales; offer/sale/deal endings tonight, tomorrow, today, at midnight or on named weekdays; weekend-only/final-day claims; “valid until” deadlines; numeric hour specials; “only until” deadlines; and “last day to claim.” The cue still contributes **two points at most once per ad**. Inventory scarcity, extreme discounts, countdowns and alarmist claims remain separately named cues with unchanged weights and tier boundaries.

The synthetic development suite checks ten explicit deadline phrasings. It also checks “We don't use limited-time offers,” “Avoid saying limited time offer,” limited opening hours, articles explaining deadlines, limited editions and “The phrase 'last chance' is misleading” as non-promotional uses. A genuine quoted headline followed by a stock claim remains detectable. Two time phrases in one ad produce one urgency contribution, not double counting. These are software tests, not measured real-ad recall.

The reserved Phase 13 real-ad split contains **zero explicit English deadline positives**. Its 10 assessable cue opportunities (five images × two predeclared cues) support a false-alarm check only: revised urgency produced no positive alert. Precision, recall and F1 are undefined because there were no true positives or predicted positives. A generated deadline image passed the real-browser OCR-to-cue path, but it cannot substitute for a real-ad holdout. The two previously inspected Phase 12 positives remain diagnostic, not fresh validation.

Unlisted wording, vague phrases such as “Friday sale,” dates without clear offer context, OCR substitutions, rhetorical quotations and multilingual deadlines remain unresolved. Obtain fresh reuse-cleared positive ads with independent cue adjudication before estimating urgency recall or expanding patterns again.
